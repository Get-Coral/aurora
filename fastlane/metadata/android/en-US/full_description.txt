Aurora is a beautiful, open-source front-end for your own Jellyfin media server. It keeps Jellyfin as the source of truth and layers a cinematic, living-room-ready experience on top.

WHAT YOU GET
• A home screen built for discovery: featured titles, Continue Watching, favourites and recommendations
• Rich detail pages with cast, related titles and full episode lists for series
• Movie and series libraries with genre browsing and sorting
• My List, synced with your Jellyfin favourites
• Fast search across your whole library
• Embedded playback that reports progress back to your server
• Subtitles from OpenSubtitles (bring your own free API key)
• Multiple profiles for shared households, with an optional sign-in per user
• A TV mode with large, remote-friendly controls
• Available in English and Dutch

YOUR SERVER, YOUR DATA
Aurora has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It talks only to the Jellyfin server you connect it to, and your settings stay on your device.

REQUIREMENTS
You need access to a Jellyfin server with a server URL, an API key and a user account. Aurora does not provide any media content.

OPEN SOURCE
