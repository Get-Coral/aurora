Aurora Cinema turns your headset into a private cinema for the films and series you already have: your own Jellyfin server and the IPTV subscription from your provider.

Browse in a comfortable panel, then step into the theater: the film plays on a big screen, and you sit in a seat of your own.

PLACES TO WATCH
• Grand Theater: red velvet and a starlit ceiling, from a VIP row
• Orbital Deck: a lounge above a planet, under a sea of stars
• Sentinel HQ: a penthouse high above a city at night
• Arcane Library: floating candles and a crackling fire
• Biolume Grove: a glowing jungle under two moons
• Northern lights, a dark room, or your own room

The room reacts to the film: press play and the lights go down; pause and they come back up.

YOUR LIBRARY AND IPTV
• Continue Watching, favourites, genres and search
• Progress syncs back to your server
• Audio and subtitle choice
• Profiles for shared households
• Your own M3U, Xtream Codes or stream URL, with a TV guide

Aurora Cinema includes no channels or media of its own: you need your own Jellyfin server or IPTV provider.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Open source at github.com/Get-Coral/aurora. Not affiliated with the Jellyfin project.
